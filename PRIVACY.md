# Privacy Policy: Document Zoom for Google Drive

*Last updated: 26 September 2026*

Document Zoom for Google Drive does not collect, store, send or share any
personal data or user data.

- The extension runs only on `docs.google.com` and `drive.google.com`. It
  listens for the zoom shortcut (for example Ctrl + Alt + mouse wheel) and
  presses the page's own zoom controls.
- It does not read, copy or send the contents of your documents.
- It makes no network requests and uses no analytics or tracking.
- The only thing it stores is your own settings: modifier keys, zoom step
  and scroll direction. They are kept in Chrome's extension storage
  (`chrome.storage.sync`). If you have Chrome sync turned on, Chrome syncs
  them between your own browsers. The developer never receives them.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/bjartesorensen/googledrivezoom/issues
